{% comment %} The status colour on its own, with no label and no picker. Rows that are mostly text — the focus strip, a feed line — want the status as a single mark rather than as a cell. The palette is the one in ``_status_cell.html``; keeping it in one include is what stops the two drifting into different blues. Expects ``status`` (a status key) and optionally ``dot_class`` for the size, defaulting to the 7px mark the focus rows use. {% endcomment %}
