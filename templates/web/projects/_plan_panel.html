The boards answer "where is the work"; this answers "how does the work sit inside what we committed to". That is two dimensions at once — a milestone holds epics, an epic spans milestones — so which one leads is the knob, and both readings stay one click apart. Rows arrive flat from ``apps.web.plan`` with a depth and a collapse key, so the whole tree is one loop and one Alpine flag per key.
