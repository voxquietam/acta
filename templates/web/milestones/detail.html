Three readings side by side, which is the shape GitLab and Jira release reports settled on: progress says how much is done, the burndown says whether that pace lands before the date, and the at-risk list names the work that will not. A number on its own has never moved anything. Below them the same work twice, sliced two ways — by project, because a shared commitment has to say whose part is whose, and by epic, because an epic whose work spans three milestones is normal and must read as normal. Then the attached work itself, including what the counting rule leaves out, so a page that says ``4/7`` and lists nine rows accounts for the other two.
