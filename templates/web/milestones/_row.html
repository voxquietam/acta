Two layouts of the same row: a grid from ``md:`` up, a stack below it. The date, the progress and what is at risk are the three things a person scans this list for, so all three survive to the phone; the per-project chips come with them, because a shared commitment that does not say whose slice is whose is just a date. The whole row is a link, drawn as a stretched anchor underneath the content rather than wrapped around it — a form (the "Close" button on a finished milestone) cannot live inside an ````. Hence ``pointer-events-none`` on the content and ``pointer-events-auto`` on the one control that has its own job. {% endcomment %}
